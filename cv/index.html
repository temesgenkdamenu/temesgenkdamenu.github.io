Professional Summary

PhD researcher at the University of Kent’s School of Computing and Institute of Cyber Security for Society (iCSS), working on cyber security and online safety education for children, including game-based learning. Over twelve years of experience in cyber security in technical and leadership roles, including leading the Cyber Security Governance and Management Division at Ethiopia’s Information Network Security Agency (INSA). CISM certified.
