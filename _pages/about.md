I am a PhD researcher in Computer Science at the [University of Kent](https://www.kent.ac.uk/), UK, and a member of the [Institute of Cyber Security for Society (iCSS)](https://research.kent.ac.uk/cyber/). I am supervised by [Professor Shujun Li](https://www.hooklee.com/) and [Dr Alexandra Covaci](https://www.kent.ac.uk/engineering-and-digital-design/people/664/covaci-alexandra). I also work as a Graduate Teaching Assistant in the School of Computing.

**Currently.** My research is on cyber security and online safety education for children and young people. I study how engaging approaches, especially game-based learning and educational games, can help children build lasting cyber security awareness and skills. I am also active in school outreach. In early 2026 I was the main deliverer of [TTT-CyberEthiopia](https://blogs.kent.ac.uk/cyber-institute-news/2026/05/03/icss-delivered-ttt-cyberethiopia-project/), a "train the trainers" project that reached over 600 schoolteachers in Addis Ababa.

**Previously.** I have over twelve years of experience in cyber security in technical and leadership roles. At Ethiopia's [Information Network Security Agency (INSA)](https://insa.gov.et/), I led the Cyber Security Governance and Management Division and, before that, the National Information Security Management Centre. I also served as Chief Security Officer at eTech S.C. and taught cyber security at [Addis Ababa University](https://www.aau.edu.et/). I hold an MSc in Information Security and IT Management from [Edge Hill University](https://www.edgehill.ac.uk/), UK, a BSc in Electrical Engineering from [Bahir Dar University](https://www.bdu.edu.et/), Ethiopia, and the Certified Information Security Manager (CISM) credential.

**Research interests**

- Cyber security and online safety education for children
- Game-based learning and serious games for security awareness
- Human and organisational aspects of cyber security
- Information security management, governance and risk
